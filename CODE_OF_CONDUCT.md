# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want tfpretty to be a welcoming, respectful place for everyone. We are committed to creating a community where people can contribute, ask questions, share ideas, and learn without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected Behavior

When taking part in this project, please:

- Be respectful and considerate of others.
- Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus discussions on improving the project and supporting the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or intimidation.
- Sharing someone else's private information without permission.
- Disrupting discussions or preventing others from participating.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers. Reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially where possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or access to the project when this Code of Conduct is not followed.
